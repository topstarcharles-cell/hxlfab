Helpful Info

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What file formats do you accept for production?

We accept standard Gerber files (RS-274X format), ODB++, and drill files. Our engineering team checks every file for free before manufacturing.

2. How can I contact the sales or engineering team directly?

You can send your inquiry, BOM lists, or Gerber files directly to our business email at sales@hxlfab.com for a prompt quotation.

3. What is the typical turnaround time for prototypes?

Standard 2-layer PCB prototypes can be manufactured and shipped within 24 to 48 hours. Multilayer boards usually take 4 to 6 days.

4. How do you ship international orders?

We partner with global courier giants like DHL, FedEx, and UPS to ensure rapid, secure door-to-door delivery worldwide.

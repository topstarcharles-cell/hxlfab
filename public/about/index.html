Your project team

Clear ownership at each stage.

Named profiles will only be published with verified job titles, company email addresses, real photographs and the team member’s approval. Until then, these are the functions responsible for the work.

01 · RFQ coordination

Sales engineering

Owns initial scope, commercial assumptions, required inputs and schedule communication.

02 · Data review

CAM & DFM engineering

Reviews fabrication data, stack-up, tooling, design rules and manufacturability questions.

03 · Process route

Process engineering

Confirms materials, via structures, plating, lamination and special-process requirements.

04 · Acceptance plan

Quality

Aligns inspection, electrical test, targeted verification and required project records.

05 · Released build

Production coordination

Tracks the released revision through the confirmed process route and shipment preparation.

06 · Customer decisions

Your technical contact

Questions and approvals are documented through the sales engineering contact for the project.

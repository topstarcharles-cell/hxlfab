Top questions

Common questions, direct answers.

These are the topics most likely to affect quote quality, revision control, or supplier trust.

Getting a quoteWhat should I send for a PCB quote?

Send the fabrication package, construction requirements, quantity, and any acceptance or commercial conditions that could change the manufacturing route.

Read answer →Stack-up & materialsWhat data is needed for controlled impedance?

An impedance value alone is incomplete; connect each target to its structure, layers, geometry, tolerance, material assumptions, and verification plan.

Read answer →Manufacturing capabilitiesHow should published capability limits be used?

A listed minimum or maximum is a planning boundary; the complete design, material, copper, tolerance, panelization, and quantity still require review.

Read answer →Quality & documentsHow do I request a quality document pack?

Ask for controlled copies and match each document to the legal entity, manufacturing site, scope, issuing body, and validity period.

Read answer →Commercial termsHow are MOQ, price, and lead time confirmed?

There is no unverified universal MOQ or lead time on the website; written terms are confirmed for the submitted construction and quantity.

Read answer →File security & onboardingWhat are the secure upload limits and file types?

The current RFQ accepts up to 10 supported files, 100 MB each and 200 MB total, and stores uploads in private access-controlled object storage.

Guidance boundary

The Help Center supports a review. It does not approve a design.

Published limits and process explanations are planning inputs. Final capability, material, price, lead time, testing, documents, shipping, and terms are confirmed against the actual RFQ.

What belongs on a fabrication drawing?

The drawing should state the finished-board requirements that are not reliably conveyed by image and drill data alone.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

In this answer

← All Files & DFM answers
Direct answer

A fabrication drawing should identify the board revision, finished dimensions, layer construction or stack-up reference, material, copper, thickness, surface finish, solder mask, legend, hole and profile requirements, acceptance criteria, testing, and any marking or packing needs.

Core drawing information

Use the drawing as the readable statement of finished-board intent and keep it consistent with the digital fabrication data.

  • Part number, revision, units, and drawing date
  • Finished outline, dimensions, cutouts, slots, and critical tolerances
  • Layer count, finished thickness, copper by layer, and stack-up reference
  • Laminate or performance requirement and permitted alternatives
  • Surface finish, solder mask, legend, and marking

Project-specific notes

Add controlled impedance, via treatment, edge features, cleanliness, electrical test, coupon, microsection, report, packaging, and compliance requirements only where they apply. Resolve conflicting boilerplate before submission.

Commercial terms

How are MOQ, price, and lead time confirmed?

There is no unverified universal MOQ or lead time on the website; written terms are confirmed for the submitted construction and quantity.

⌕

Direct answer

HXLFAB does not publish a universal MOQ, instant price, quote response time, or production lead time. These are confirmed after reviewing file completeness, construction, material availability, quantity, panel utilization, testing, special processes, capacity, and commercial terms.

What affects the answer

Prototype, pilot, and production quantities can follow different panel, test, material, and process assumptions. A useful quotation records the conditions behind its price and schedule.

  • Board size, construction, copper, and panel utilization
  • Material availability and permitted alternatives
  • HDI, RF, impedance, heavy-copper, or special processes
  • Test, reports, samples, marking, and packing
  • Quantity, target date, production load, and commercial terms

What to confirm in writing

Check quantity and price break, quoted revision, material and process assumptions, included tests and records, validity, production lead-time basis, shipment terms, and conditions that trigger requotation.

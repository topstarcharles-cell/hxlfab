Getting a quote

What happens after I submit an RFQ?

The request receives a traceable RFQ record, then the available files and requirements are reviewed before technical and commercial assumptions are confirmed.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

In this answer

Direct answer

The website creates a traceable RFQ record. The supplied data is checked for completeness, engineering questions are raised where needed, and the proposed process and commercial assumptions are aligned before a quotation is confirmed. Quote timing depends on file completeness and technical complexity; no universal response time is promised on the website.

Review sequence

The exact route varies with board construction, but the same control principle applies: the quote should correspond to a known revision and a defined manufacturing scope.

  • RFQ and upload intake
  • File-completeness and construction review
  • CAM, DFM, material, test, or documentation questions
  • Engineer-confirmed process assumptions
  • Written price, schedule, and commercial conditions

How to reduce clarification cycles

Reply with one consolidated set of answers where practical, identify updated files clearly, and state which earlier assumptions are superseded. Do not rely on an email attachment name alone as revision control.

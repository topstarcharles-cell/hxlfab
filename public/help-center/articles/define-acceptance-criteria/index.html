Quality & documents

How should acceptance criteria be defined?

Identify the governing standard and class, then add only the drawing and customer-specific criteria needed for the actual product.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

← All Quality & documents answers
Direct answer

State the applicable acceptance standard and class, approved drawing and revision, dimensions and tolerances, material and construction, electrical and targeted test requirements, visual or workmanship criteria, sampling, records, and any customer-specific specification. Resolve conflicts before release.

Build a clear hierarchy

Define which document controls if the purchase order, drawing, data, industry standard, and customer specification differ. Avoid broad boilerplate that conflicts with the actual construction.

  • Governing industry standard and class
  • Customer specification and revision
  • Fabrication drawing and approved data revision
  • Critical dimensional, electrical, material, and cosmetic criteria
  • Sampling, test, report, marking, and packaging requirements
  • Authorized deviation and approval process

Confirm feasibility before purchase release

Engineering and quality should confirm that the acceptance plan matches the quoted process. Unstated criteria introduced after quotation may require a revised route, price, or schedule.

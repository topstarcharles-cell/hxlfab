Which project quality records can I request?

Records depend on the agreed build and control plan; identify required evidence before quotation so availability, format, and cost can be confirmed.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

In this answer

Direct answer

Depending on the agreed construction and control plan, a project package may include electrical-test records, impedance measurements, microsection results, material declarations, or a certificate of conformance. Availability is not automatic; confirm each required record, format, lot relationship, and delivery point in writing at quotation.

Define the evidence

Name the record and what decision it supports. A generic request for a full report package can leave scope, sampling, and acceptance unclear.

  • Record or certificate name
  • Applicable standard, drawing note, or customer procedure
  • Lot, panel, coupon, or serial relationship
  • Sampling plan and acceptance criteria
  • Raw data, summary, signed certificate, or customer-template format
  • Required delivery timing and retention expectation

Include it in the quotation

Reports, coupons, destructive testing, special samples, serialization, and customer portals may affect process and commercial scope. Confirm them before production release.

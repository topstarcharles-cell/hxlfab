Manufacturing capabilities

What is needed for a heavy-copper review?

Specify copper by layer and review it together with conductor geometry, thermal intent, dielectric fill, holes, plating, and finished thickness.

Direct answer

Provide copper weight for every layer, minimum conductor width and clearance, current and thermal intent, copper distribution, stack-up, material, finished thickness, hole and plating requirements, surface finish, quantity, and acceptance criteria. HXLFAB lists capability up to 4 oz on inner layers and 8 oz on outer layers, subject to design review.

Review the construction as a system

Heavy copper changes etch compensation, spacing, resin fill, copper balance, hole-wall requirements, and overall thickness. Standard one-ounce rules should not be assumed.

  • Copper weight and geometry by layer
  • Current, temperature-rise, or thermal-spreading intent
  • Dielectric, resin-fill, and symmetry constraints
  • Finished-hole and plating requirement
  • Surface finish, electrical test, and microsection criteria where required

Separate capability from electrical validation

Manufacturing review can identify process constraints, but conductor sizing and thermal performance remain product-design decisions. Provide the approved fabrication geometry rather than asking the fabricator to infer the electrical requirement.

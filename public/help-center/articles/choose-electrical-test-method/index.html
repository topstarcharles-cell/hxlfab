How is the electrical test method selected?

The test route is selected for the agreed build and acceptance plan; do not assume one method or report format applies to every order.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

Direct answer

Flying-probe, fixture, or four-wire electrical testing may be selected according to the agreed board, quantity, requirements, and control plan. Provide a suitable netlist or test data where required and state coverage, voltage/current conditions, Kelvin or resistance needs, and report expectations before quotation.

Inputs for test planning

The fabrication package should define the intended connectivity and any requirements beyond a standard open/short test.

  • Approved IPC-356 or other usable netlist when applicable
  • Open/short coverage and acceptance threshold
  • Four-wire or low-resistance requirement
  • Test exclusions, special networks, or high-voltage needs
  • Report, serialization, or traceability requirement
  • Quantity and prototype-to-volume intent

Confirm the quoted control plan

The quotation should state the included test method and any required report or fixture assumption. Electrical testing of bare-board connectivity does not replace functional testing of the assembled product.

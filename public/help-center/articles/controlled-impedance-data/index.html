Stack-up & materials

What data is needed for controlled impedance?

An impedance value alone is incomplete; connect each target to its structure, layers, geometry, tolerance, material assumptions, and verification plan.

✓ Reviewed August 15, 2026
⌕

Direct answer

For each controlled structure, state the target value and tolerance, single-ended or differential type, signal layer, reference layer or layers, nominal trace width and spacing, copper requirement, material or dielectric assumptions, and coupon or measurement requirements.

Build an impedance table

Use a unique identifier for each structure and apply the same identifier in the layout notes, stack-up, and drawing.

  • Target impedance and permitted tolerance
  • Single-ended, differential, coplanar, or other structure
  • Signal and reference layers
  • Nominal line width and differential spacing
  • Copper and dielectric assumptions
  • Coupon quantity, location, measurement, and report needs

Geometry adjustments need agreement

Manufacturing compensation or a proposed stack-up may require geometry changes. Define whether adjustment is permitted and who can approve it. Testing is not assumed for every order; it should be included in the agreed quotation and fabrication requirements.

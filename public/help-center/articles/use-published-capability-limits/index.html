Manufacturing capabilities

How should published capability limits be used?

A listed minimum or maximum is a planning boundary; the complete design, material, copper, tolerance, panelization, and quantity still require review.

Direct answer

Use published capability values to decide whether a design is worth submitting for review. Do not combine every minimum and maximum into an assumed production rule. Final feasibility, price, and schedule are confirmed from the actual data, construction, material, and quantity.

Current planning envelope

The website lists rigid PCB fabrication from 1 to 30 layers, down to 3/3 mil trace and space, 0.15 mm minimum mechanical drill, 0.10 mm minimum laser via, up to 4 oz inner and 8 oz outer copper, up to an 850 × 520 mm panel, and 0.15–7.0 mm finished thickness.

Why limits cannot be read independently

Copper weight changes etching and spacing. Finished thickness and hole size affect drilling and plating. Material affects lamination and fine features. Panel utilization, tolerances, via structure, test, and volume also influence the practical route.

  • Submit the full design near a boundary
  • Identify the features that cannot change
  • Allow engineering to propose alternatives only where permitted
  • Require written confirmation before design release

What should I verify during supplier onboarding?

Match the HXLFAB brand and supplied company profile to current legal, site, certificate, contracting, banking, and project-contact documents.

Direct answer

HXLFAB is the customer-facing brand. The supplied company profile names Guilin Hengxinlong Electronic Technology Co., Ltd., a Guilin production base, and a Shenzhen service office. Use current registration, certificate, contract, banking, and site documents—not this web page alone—to verify the responsible legal and operating entities for your project.

Onboarding checks

Ask for the controlled documents required by your procurement and quality process, and resolve differences in English or Chinese names, addresses, covered sites, scope, and responsible entity.

  • Legal registration and contracting entity
  • Manufacturing and service-site role
  • Certificate entity, address, scope, issuer, and validity
  • Purchase-order, invoice, and verified banking details
  • Sales-engineering, quality, and escalation contacts
  • NDA and customer-data responsibilities

Published operating locations

The website identifies the production base at Jinjiping Industrial Park, Xinping Town, Lipu City, Guilin, Guangxi, China, and the service office at Room 911, Haichuan Building, Baoyuan Road, Xixiang, Bao'an District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China. Confirm current legal addresses in project documents before supplier registration or audit planning.
